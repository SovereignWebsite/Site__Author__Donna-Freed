# Site__Author__Donna-Freed
donna-freed.author.sovereignweb.site: Writer and translator. Her books, her writing and her podcast.

A public author site for Donna Freed, who has agreed to it (Dinis Cruz, 22 November 2026). It is a draft for her approval.

**Not for the hub.** This site is not to be listed on the Sovereign Website hub (Dinis Cruz, 22 November 2026: "not in hub"). It carries no hub.json.

Rules for a real person's site: no photograph without written permission from her or the photographer; no contact details, address, family, age or anything personal unless she gives it for the site; every claim cites its source and the date it was read (see /about/); nothing is written in her voice unless it is her own words.

Pages are written by `python3 docs/tools/make-pages.py` into `site/`. Five designs (choose-a-design control on every page) come from `site/assets/site.css` and `site/assets/design.js`.
