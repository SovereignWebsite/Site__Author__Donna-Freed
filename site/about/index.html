About Donna Freed

Everything here is a claim from a public source, named below. It becomes her biography only when she writes or approves it.

  • A native of New York, a writer and a translator. Jewish Book Council profile; Google Books author overview.
  • Her Kafka translation has been in print since 1996. Jewish Book Council profile.
  • Has written for The Oldie and the BBC, and co-presented Radio Gorgeous. Jewish Book Council profile.
  • Has lived in London since 2005. Google Books author overview only, not yet confirmed by a second source.
