Writer and translator

Donna Freed

A New York native, a writer and a translator. Her translation of Kafka has been in print since 1996. She has written for The Oldie and the BBC, and co-presented the Radio Gorgeous podcast.

Source: Jewish Book Council author profile, read 22 November 2026.

Books

Duplicity: My Mothers' Secrets, a memoir from Muswell Press, and her translation of Kafka's The Metamorphosis and Other Stories.

Podcast

Radio Gorgeous, where she has talked with writers about their books.

Writing

Her journalism, for The Oldie and the BBC. Links to follow when she chooses them.

About this draft

This is a first version, made so that Donna can see what a site of her own could be. The five designs above are five ways to read the same pages. Her photographs, her own words and the links she wants are what will make it hers; the questions page says what is needed.
