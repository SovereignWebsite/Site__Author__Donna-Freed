Questions

Questions for Donna

This is a draft site for you to shape. Each answer below makes it more yours.

  1. Your words. A short biography in your own voice, and a line about each book. We will use nothing we have written as yours without your approval.
  2. Photographs. Which photographs would you like, and do you give written permission for them? If another person took one, we need theirs. We use no picture of other people without their consent.
  3. Your books. Which titles, in which editions? Is Duplicity described, and in what words? Which spelling of the title is right?
  4. Writing and the podcast. Which articles and episodes do you want shown, with which links?
  5. Other work. Translations, talks, festivals or anything else you want listed.
  6. Links. Which accounts and pages, if any, may we link? We add none that you do not choose.
  7. Reaching you. Do you want a way for readers to contact you or your publisher, and which? We add no contact details unless you give them.
  8. The look. Which of the five designs do you like, or what would you change?

Tell the person who made the site, or open an issue in the repository. You can edit every page yourself: see Start here.
